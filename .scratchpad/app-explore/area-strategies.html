Recession Radar · version 3 · 12 lines
CopyOpen in editor
(strategy "Recession Radar" :rebalance monthly :benchmark SPY (if (> (rsi SPY 14) 70) (weight :method specified (asset TLT :weight 60) (asset GLD :weight 40)) (else (weight :method momentum :lookback 90 :top 2 (asset VTI) (asset QQQ) (asset IWM)))))
Named Recession Radar. Trades on the first trading day of each month. Results are compared with holding SPY. When SPY's 14-day RSI is above 70, the market looks stretched. Then use fixed weights: 60% long-term treasuries, 40% gold. Any other time: rank by 90-day return, keep the top 2, from total US market, Nasdaq 100, and small caps.
Valid. Last checked when saved on Nov 3.Needs 90 trading days of history.The visual builder and Copilot both write this same code.
