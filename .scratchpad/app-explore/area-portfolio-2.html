How live money moves
LlamaTrade never moves real money in or out. Deposits and withdrawals happen in Alpaca.
When a check finds more cash in Alpaca than the ledger expects and no order explains it, the ledger records a deposit to unallocated.
To withdraw, move money from a sleeve to unallocated here first, then withdraw that amount in Alpaca, so the two keep agreeing.
Recording a deposit automatically needs Alpaca's account activities. Today the difference would be flagged by the check instead.
