Template · daily · benchmark SPY
Recession Radar
Holds long bonds and gold while SPY looks overbought, otherwise the strongest of three sector funds by 90 day momentum.
Run a backtest
Rulesrecession-radar.llt
(strategy "Recession Radar" :rebalance :daily :benchmark SPY (if (> (rsi SPY 14) 70) ; overbought: step aside (weight :equal TLT GLD) ; otherwise: strongest sector (filter :top 1 (momentum 90) XLK SMH IWM)))
In plain words
1Each day, check SPY's 14 day RSI.
2Above 70, hold TLT and GLD in equal parts.
3Otherwise hold whichever of XLK, SMH or IWM rose most over 90 days.
This is the whole strategy. A backtest runs it over past prices, with no broker involved.
Last published backtestOct 2021 to Oct 2026 · $25,000 start · vs SPY
