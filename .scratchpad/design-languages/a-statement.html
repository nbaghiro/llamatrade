One language

The blocks, the code and the copilot all edit the same strategy.

A strategy is a short program that returns target weights, for example 60% TLT and 40% GLD. It never says "buy 100 shares". LlamaTrade works out the orders from the weights, your holdings and your sleeve's cash, selling before buying so a rebalance funds itself.

The language has 17 indicators, six weighting methods and conditions across symbols. It refuses anything the engine can't run, so a strategy that saves is a strategy that trades.

Blocks

Nested weights and conditions, edited as an outline.

Code

The same strategy as text, with line-level validation.

Copilot

Describe it in plain English, then review the result before saving.

1(strategy "Recession Radar"
2  :rebalance monthly
3  :benchmark SPY
4  (if (> (rsi SPY 14) 70)
5      (weight :method specified
6        (asset TLT :weight 60)
7        (asset GLD :weight 40))
8      (else
9        (weight :method momentum :lookback 90 :top 2
10          (asset VTI)
11          (asset QQQ)
12          (asset IWM)))))

Fig. 2When RSI(SPY, 14) is above 70, hold treasuries and gold. Otherwise hold the two strongest of three equity funds, weighted by 90-day return.

Book of record

Every dollar has an account, and every entry balances.

Alpaca sees one cash balance and one position per symbol. LlamaTrade keeps a ledger on top of that, so each strategy has its own capital, its own lots and its own P&L, even when two strategies hold the same fund.

The ledger is checked against Alpaca on a schedule. If they disagree on a quantity, the affected sleeves stop trading and you're told why. Nothing is adjusted on your behalf.2

Notes

The idea, and what building it would take

Why a statement

LlamaTrade's core promise is that money state is correct: a double-entry ledger, sleeves that can't touch each other, and reconciliation that stops trading rather than guess. A brokerage statement is the existing visual form of that promise, and most people who trade have read one.

Its conventions already carry meaning: right-aligned tabular figures, parentheses for losses, a single rule for a subtotal and a double rule for a total, numbered schedules and footnotes. Footnotes give honest limitations a permanent place on the page, such as the IEX data coverage, rather than leaving them out.

Colour stays narrow. Navy is the only accent, and it never touches a number's sign. Green and red are reserved for P&L. Amber appears only when something needs the person's attention, and it always comes with a sentence.

Building it in the current app

  • The Monolith token layer in apps/web/src/styles/monolith.css already drives Tailwind through CSS variables, so the palette, fonts and radius change in one file. Shadows go to none, radius to 2 px, and new semantic tokens are added for gain, loss, attention and the accent.
  • Most component work is removing classes: about 66 component files use the hard shadows, about 99 use uppercase mono labels and about 83 use the orange ramp. These become the label, figure and rule patterns above.
  • Three small shared pieces: a figure formatter in apps/core/src/format.ts for signs, parentheses and decimals; a schedule table with subtotal and total rows; and a footnote component.
  • The hand-written SVG charts switch from green or red by direction to the navy strategy line, and gain a crosshair. The strategy identity palette drops its green and red entries so they no longer collide with P&L.
  • Dark mode is currently retired. The preset is variable-based, so a [data-theme="dark"] block plus the existing toggle brings it back. The React Native tokens in apps/core/src/theme/tokens.ts mirror the same values.
