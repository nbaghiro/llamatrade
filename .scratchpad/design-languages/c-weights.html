Copilot

Describe a strategy in a sentence. Review the weights before you save.

The copilot writes in the same strategy language as the block editor, and every proposal is checked by the validator before you see it. Nothing is saved or run until you accept it.

You can open the proposal in the editor, change a weight or a condition, and backtest it from there.

Hold the two strongest of QQQ, XLK and SMH by 90-day momentum, equal weight, rebalanced monthly.
(strategy "Tech Momentum Rotation"
  :rebalance monthly :benchmark QQQ
  (filter :by momentum :select (top 2) :lookback 90
    (weight :method equal
      (asset QQQ) (asset XLK) (asset SMH))))

Rebalancing

Weights drift between rebalances. LlamaTrade brings them back on schedule.

On each rebalance date the strategy is evaluated again, and the sleeve's holdings are compared with the new targets. Sells go first, so the proceeds fund the buys in the same rebalance.

A drift band can skip trades that are too small to matter. Between rebalance dates nothing trades, however far prices move.

Notes

The idea, and what building it would take

A strategy is a set of weights

LlamaTrade's strategy language never says "buy 100 shares". It returns target weights, and sizing turns those into orders. The backend's sleeves are weights too: each strategy owns a share of the account.

This direction makes that the visual system. One primitive, a bar that sums to 100%, appears at every level: the account, a sleeve, a weight block, a template preview, a month of backtest history. Target versus actual is a tick on a bar, so drift is visible without reading a number.

It's the most approachable of the three and the easiest to explain on a marketing page. It's also the least dense, and the five-step ink ramp limits how many holdings a single bar can show before they fold into "Other".

Building it in the current app

  • Theme: new values in monolith.css, Geist and Geist Mono instead of Anton, Archivo and Space Mono, radius tokens from 0 to 12, 9, 6 and 3, and shadows replaced by one soft elevation.
  • A shared WeightBar component in the web app, fed by the strategy's evaluated targets and the sleeve's holdings. The portfolio store already has positions per sleeve; targets per sleeve would need to be read from the latest rebalance.
  • The editor's blocks (StrategyTree, strategy-builder/blocks) gain a bar per weight block. Showing which branch applies today needs an evaluation preview from the strategy or backtest service, which the editor doesn't call today.
  • The monthly allocation columns need the backtest result to include holdings per rebalance. The backtest stores an equity curve and trades; per-rebalance weights would be a new field or derived from the trades.
  • Dark mode comes back through a [data-theme] block, as in the other directions.
