Choose · Strategies and the builder

Pick one design for each strategy screen

Each screen below is drawn two to four ways, with the desktop app in a browser frame and the phone beside it. The recommended option opens first, and the note under it gives the idea, the trade-off and what it would take to build.

The data is the closed beta account used across these pages. The latest backtests run from Nov 2024 to Oct 2026, where Recession Radar returned +21.4% and SPY returned +24.9%.

How to use this page
  • ←→Switch between options, or click a tab.
  • PPick the option on screen. Press it again to clear the pick.
  • JKMove to the next or previous screen.
  • SavedPicks are kept in this browser and shared with the other choose pages. The counter in the corner shows how many are left, and All picks lists them together.
  • LookTheme, accent and font are in the bar at the top. Phones for screens the mobile app doesn't have yet are marked as proposed.
