Merged and left out

  • Verify email and choose a new password share one surface, Pages opened from an email, since they're the same kind of page with different states.
  • The Alpaca callback (/oauth/alpaca/callback) only shows "Signing you in" for a moment, so it's part of Returning from Alpaca sign-up rather than its own screen.
  • Subscribe and payment methods (/billing/subscribe, /billing/payment-methods) are folded into Plan and usage. During the beta there's nothing to pay, so they'd only show empty forms.
  • The Markets tab on Home's chart isn't a separate choice. It's a tab on the equity panel in every Home option.
  • The phone Account tab is shown as the phone frame of Settings, profile and security.
  • Empty Sessions and Backtests follow the Strategies empty state, so they aren't repeated.
  • Dropped from the earlier flows: the blurred product behind sign-in, which read as a loading state, and the Home setup bar as a separate onboarding route, which is now First visit option A.
