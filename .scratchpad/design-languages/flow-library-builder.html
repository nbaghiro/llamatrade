Product flows · Direction B, Session

Strategy library and builder

Two flows, drawn several ways each so a final plan can be chosen per screen. Every variation uses the same account and the same six strategies as the screens page, so the differences come from layout, density and hierarchy rather than from the data.

Each variation shows the desktop web app and the phone beside it, with a short note on its idea, what it costs and what building it would take. One variation per screen is marked as recommended, and the plan at the end puts the picks together.

What the drawings hold to
  • The data is the real product's: the strategy language, its weight methods and its 17 indicators.
  • Green and red mark profit and loss only. Problems and frozen states use amber, and the accent marks what applies or is selected.
  • Code and diffs use the accent for added lines and a hatch for removed ones.
  • The mobile app has no editor today, so builder phones are proposals and say so in their captions.

Final plan

The recommended variation for each screen, with what it borrows from the others. Together they make one library and one editor that share a vocabulary: the outline, the weight bars and the "would hold today" figure.
