Sign-in, first run and Home, three or four ways each

These are design options for the first three flows a person meets: signing in or creating an account, a first visit that ends on a real backtest, and Home. Each variation is drawn in the B Session language, with the desktop web app at 1440 by 900 beside the mobile app, using the same account and figures as the screen drawings.

The variations differ in layout, density and how the product shows up. Tabs above a variation move through its screens, and the bar at the top switches theme and accent. One variation per flow is marked as recommended, and the plan at the end proposes a direction for each.
